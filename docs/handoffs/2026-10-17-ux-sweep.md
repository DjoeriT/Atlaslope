# UX and UI sweep (17 Oct 2026)

Owner's ask (15:26Z): "a UX/UI review before we hit content: a full sweep on best practice UX, navigation, transitions".
Branch `claude/project-thread-fjyj5w`.

**Found.** 20 findings, 4 of them P1 (`Review/ux-sweep-1-findings.md`), from 66 screenshots at desktop, iPad, phone and phone
landscape, light and dark, plus a click-through and a quiz round.

**Decided (owner cards, 15:58Z).** Full motion set; the phone card becomes a sheet with three drag stops; the study region that holds
only its namesake place (Rioja) is skipped. Default taken: the Progress tab is hidden until the mastery map ships. Motion retuned after
"jittery and too fast" (16:28Z); "OK better" on preview v2 (16:44Z).

**Built.**
1. Motion (`src/app/motion.ts`, `src/styles/ux.css`): screen changes run as view transitions. Cards crossfade and rise (0.48 s), a page
   slides up over the map and slides down on Back (0.6 s), the cover photo travels between a card and its page, quiz steps crossfade.
   The map flies only after the card has settled (`afterMotion`), which removed the stutter. Nothing moves under reduced motion or in
   browsers without view transitions (Firefox today).
2. Phone sheet (`src/app/Sheet.tsx`): peek (title and main button), half, full; drag, tap or arrow keys on the handle; peek hides the photo
   and takes the hidden part out of the keyboard's reach; map buttons and framing follow the sheet's top edge. One top row on phones (logo
   mark, search, two tabs), no zoom buttons (pinch).
3. Rioja: `tree.soleSameName`; trails read Spain › Rioja › Rioja Alta; old `#region=rioja` links open the place; search lists the place only.
4. One page bar for place and region pages (`src/pages/PageTop.tsx`); the region page now covers the legend and map buttons.
5. Quiz: End round asks first (Keep going is the default); an early stop says "Round stopped" with how far you got, not "All done for
   today". The quiz home lost its contradictory last line, and new place names show their first form only.
6. Smaller: "Open the X page" on place and region cards; region card has a close button and no "All of Spain"; Escape closes a page or
   steps up a card; no blue ring on titles the app focuses; desktop cards are as tall as their content; "Search" placeholder on narrow screens.

**Checks.** Design check (ReviewBot, `Review/ux-sweep-1-design-check.md`): 0 blockers, 5 should-fix, all fixed. Unit tests updated for the
Rioja step (259 pass); old UI tests moved to the new wording; `tests/ui/ux-sweep.spec.ts` covers End round, the shared page bar, Escape,
the region card, motion on/off, and the phone sheet.
