About AtlaSlope

I'm Djoeri, a passionate techie who also loves wine.

The more I studied wine, the more I wanted to know about the place behind each glass.

The facts are all there, in official documents. They just aren't easy to explore.

So I built AtlaSlope: official data, wrapped in an experience that makes you want to keep exploring.

It starts with Spain. Over the next few months it will grow, and expand into other countries.

Feedback is very welcome, from wherever in the world you are.

Cheers,
Djoeri Timessen

WSET Level 2 and Level 3. Now studying for the Spanish Wine Scholar.

Where the facts come from

Every fact in a guide comes from the place's own rulebook: the pliego de condiciones and the EU single document.

Each one is cited to its page.

Boundaries come from Spain's Ministry of Agriculture (MAPA).
