A wine atlas for anyone who loves wine.

AtlaSlope shows Spain's wine places from above, and why each one tastes the way it does. It is free, in your browser, with no account.

How to use it

  1. Find the place. Open the atlas and fly from Spain down to a single appellation.
  2. Read why. Its guide explains how the land and the weather shape the wine. Every place it names is one tap away on the map.
  3. Test yourself, if you like. Short Study rounds on the map help it stick. What you miss comes back sooner.

Where the facts come from

Boundaries and the list of appellations come from Spain's Ministry of Agriculture (MAPA).

Facts about each place come from its own rulebook: the pliego de condiciones and the EU single document. Each fact is cited to the page it comes from.

Imagery: Esri, Maxar, with PNOA © IGN for Spain. Photos are credited where they appear.

Independent

AtlaSlope is independent.

It is useful for WSET and Wine Scholar courses, but it is not affiliated with or endorsed by WSET or the Wine Scholar Guild. It uses none of their course text, maps or images.
