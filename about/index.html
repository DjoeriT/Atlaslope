A wine atlas made for studying.

AtlaSlope is a free study atlas for WSET and Wine Scholar students, starting with Spain. The maps, guides and study rounds run in your browser, with no account.

How to study with it

  1. Find the place. Open the atlas and fly from Spain to a region, then to a single appellation.
  2. Read why. Its guide explains the land and the climate behind the wine, and flies you to every place it names.
  3. Make it stick. Study it in short rounds on the map. What you miss comes back sooner.

Where the facts come from

Boundaries and the list of appellations come from Spain's Ministry of Agriculture (MAPA). Facts about each place come from its EU single document and its pliego de condiciones, cited to the page where they're used. Imagery: Esri, Maxar, with PNOA © IGN for Spain. Photos are credited where they appear.

Independent

AtlaSlope is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by WSET or the Wine Scholar Guild, and it uses none of their course text, maps or images.
