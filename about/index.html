About

I'm Djoeri, a passionate techie who also loves wine.

The more I studied wine, the more I wanted to know about the place behind each glass. So I built AtlaSlope: official data, wrapped in an experience that makes you want to keep exploring.

It starts with Spain. Over the next few months it will grow, and expand into other countries. Feedback is very welcome, from wherever in the world you are. I hope to build it out into something that helps wine lovers across the globe discover more about what's in their glass.

Cheers,
Djoeri Timessen

WSET Level 2 and Level 3. Now studying for the Spanish Wine Scholar.
