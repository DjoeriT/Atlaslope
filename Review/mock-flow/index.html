Open decisions

Four rules the screens assume. Each goes to you as a card in the thread, with my default marked.

  1. How fast new places come in

    Spaced memory says when to re-ask, not when to add. Today needs a rule, for example five new places a session, in course order.

  2. What “known” means on the mastery map

    Four shades need four thresholds. My default is right on the last two tries for known, and three spaced passes for solid.

  3. Do zones join the quiz?

    Rioja's three zones and Priorat's villages are now in scope. Do they get asked like DOs, or only after the DOCa itself is known?

  4. Course order

    “Continue Castilla y León” assumes an order through the ten regions. The Spanish Wine Scholar order, or yours to pick?
