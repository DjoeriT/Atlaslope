Credits: complete, but grow to two lines and on phone are cut ("Source: IGME," truncated) [V].
5. **Phone.** A 240 px strip is usable only for broad stops. Real map height is ~130 px (stop bar, tag and credit take the rest) [V] (`ph-c-open`, `ph-e-soils`). Stop 1 frames Logroño only: Haro and Alfaro are outside the crop, so the camera is tuned for the desktop shape [V]. Geology colours barely visible in the strip. See F5.
6. **Speed** (live, cold, no cache, `#place=rioja&page&story`, headless): DOM ready 0.2 s; map element 0.6 s; canvas 0.84 s; first pin 5.2 s; tile traffic ends ~14 s; 292 tile requests; main `atlas-*.js` 603 KB (gz) loads before the map [V, ran]. Throttled to ~1.6 Mbps/150 ms: DOM ready 4.3 s, pins not seen in 60 s [V, ran; swiftshader makes it worse]. The Rioja guide also fetches the Priorat tour file (1 KB) [V]. Tour data itself is small (names 2 KB, rivers, slope png 14 s timeline, 150 KB limit ok).
7. **Cheap default tour.** A default (camera from the stop's point or area, climate and slope only where the text is about them) would reproduce stops 1, 3-6 and the Siurana river well. It would lose, so these stay hand-tuned: (a) pitch, bearing and distance for walls/slopes (Sierra de Cantabria needs a low camera from the south; defaults tend to be too close or flat); (b) the stop's camera height versus imagery resolution (Haro station quarter: a point stop needs a zoom cap ~ imagery limit, not a fixed zoom); (c) which stops deserve a map at all (barrel/rules text has none: should be skipped, not defaulted to an overview); (d) geology stops need a key and a crop that avoids the coverage edge; (e) phone framing (fit area to aspect ratio). Camera from a polygon via fit-bounds with aspect-aware padding, plus zoom cap and skip-if-no-place, covers most of the rest.
