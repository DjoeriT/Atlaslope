# Release 21: what to test

## General (every release)
Open https://atlaslope.com/ in Chrome or Safari on a Mac or iPad, and once on your phone. Reload once with a hard refresh if a page looks stale. Anything odd: a screenshot plus the page address and what you expected is enough.

## This release
Website slice 2 (plan `Review/website-plan.md`): a home page, the atlas at its own address under the site's top bar, and Quiz renamed Study.

1. **Home page.** atlaslope.com opens on "Know the land behind the glass.", the atlas on satellite beside it, then the sources, three screens and the Rioja guide. No map loads here, so it should appear at once. "Open the atlas" and "Read the guide" lead into the atlas.
2. **The bar.** On the home page, the atlas and every place page: the black band with the logo, Atlas, Study and Search. The logo always takes you home. Guides and About are not there yet (your card: they come with their pages).
3. **The atlas at /atlas/.** atlaslope.com/atlas/ is the map as before, with the bar on top. Cards, the map menu and the legend sit under it, nothing hidden behind it. Check the map still has enough room on your laptop and phone.
4. **Old links still work.** Paste an old link such as `atlaslope.com/#place=toro`: it lands on Toro in the atlas.
5. **Study.** Every "Quiz" now reads "Study" (tab, card heading, "Back to Study"). The address is `#study`. Save or load progress moved from the logo's menu to the bottom of the Study screen.
6. **Icons and link preview.** The browser tab and the Home Screen icon are champagne and black. Paste atlaslope.com into WhatsApp or iMessage: the card shows the tagline and the satellite map.
