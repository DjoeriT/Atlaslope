Atlaslope

Nine screens on a real map, three new in 2.2: satellite imagery and 3D terrain load live (needs internet), place names and rivers are real, DO boundaries are the official MAPA layer (2014 edition) and the Rioja zones are built from the official municipality lists. Use the layers button for Satellite or Atlas base map and the half-circle button for light or dark chrome. Click through in order.

  • Explore · SpainWhole country on satellite and 3D relief, Rioja selected, floating facts card.
  • Explore · Rioja in detailTilted 3D view, three zones, soil layer, photo pins.
  • Quiz · new quizPick area, question types and length.
  • Quiz · find it on the mapNames hidden, tap the DO, confirm.
  • Quiz · what is this?Flies to the DO, four choices.
  • Quiz · resultsScore, misses circled where they really are, overall progress.
  • Quiz · region, climate or soil (new in 2.2)The exam-shaped question: the map shows the place, you answer the fact.
  • Rioja lesson (new in 2.2)Seven stops flown through in 3D, a structured card each, then find them on the map.
  • Explore · elevation profile (new in 2.2)A line across Rioja, the real altitude under it, zones and slopes.
