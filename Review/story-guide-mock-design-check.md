# Story guide mock v1: design check (ReviewBot, 18 Oct 2026)

**Verdict: fix first.** 2 must fix, 7 should fix, 5 nits. Read: story.css, story.js, README, visual-style.md, build.py (grep), all contact sheets. Not run: keyboard pass in a live browser. Ran nothing.

## Must fix
1. **The map stops following the text while tiles load.** story.js:43 and :81 return early on `!map.isStyleLoaded()`. In MapLibre that is false whenever any tile is loading, so a fast scroll is dropped. `current` is already set at :77, so `pick()` never retries. Evidence: contact-ipad-light and contact-ipad-dark show chips "Sierra de Cantabria", "Rioja Alta" and "Rioja Alavesa" over the unchanged intro view and outline. The same check silently drops a theme switch (the outline colour stays white on dark, as in contact-ipad-dark). Fix: set `let ready=false; map.on('load',()=>{ready=true;...})`, test `ready` instead of `isStyleLoaded()`, and call `paintTheme()` after `load` and on every toggle. Only set `current` once the camera call is made.
2. **Chapter links land under the map on phone and iPad.** story.css:15 only clears the 3.5rem top bar, but the map is sticky below it at 42svh (:115). A tap on a table-of-contents entry hides the chapter heading behind the map. Fix: inside the 61rem query, `html{scroll-padding-top:calc(var(--top) + 42svh + 1rem)}`.

## Should fix
3. **Two controls mean nearly the same thing.** Each card has "Show on the map" (build.py:128, :176) while the map already follows the text, and the map has "Back to the story view" (:103). That breaks "one way out, one main action" in visual-style.md. Fix: drop the per-step buttons, make the stop card a plain label with no button, and keep only the recentre pill. Also, the stop-btn pill looks like a tag, not a button (no affordance except hover). Alternative: keep the buttons but call them "Show on the map" everywhere, including the recentre pill.
4. **Large empty gaps between steps.** `.step{min-height:50vh}` (:38) leaves 150 to 250 px of blank page. contact-d-light rows 2 and 3 look like a loading failure. The accent bar at left:-3rem (:44) also runs through the blank space against the window edge. Fix: `min-height:0`, with `padding-bottom:30vh` only on the last step. Keep the bar the height of the content (`bottom:3rem`).
5. **Phone map takes too much.** 42svh plus the 56 px bar leaves about 330 px of text on a 667 px phone, and less at large text. Fix: `@media (max-width:61rem) and (max-height:46rem){.mapwrap{height:34svh}}`. Also check 200% text on iPad, where the map plus bar passes half the screen.
6. **The stop card repeats the sentence above it.** "Haro to Alfaro" and "Station quarter" quote the paragraph again. The chip repeats the card label too. It is the json caption, so keep it, but drop the `aria-live` chip, or at least set `aria-live="off"`, so a screen reader does not announce a chip at every scroll step (:248). The chip is visual only.
7. **Uppercase labels outside kickers.** `.lab` (CLIMATE, WINE, PHOTOS; :50) is uppercase ink3 at 0.72rem. The style page allows uppercase only for kickers. Fix: sentence case at 0.9rem, weight 600.
8. **Off-scale type.** `.stop-btn small` is 0.7rem (:80) and `.maplibregl-ctrl-attrib` is 0.7rem (:110); the scale floor is 0.78. Buttons use 0.92, 1.05 and 1.1 rem where the scale has 0.9 and 1.0 (:23, :69, :77, :103). Fix: use 0.78, 0.9 and 1.0, and use 1.25 for `.stop .cap`.
9. **Zone labels on the map are weak.** `.mk.zl` (:105) is white text with a text-shadow over light terrain; "Rioja Alta" is hard to read in contact-d-light row 3. Fix: a stronger shadow, or the white pill style of `.mk`, which also works in dark mode.
