# Home page benchmark: ten comparable sites (18 Oct 2026)

I looked at live first-screen screenshots of every site at 1440×900. Two sites refused the cloud browser, JancisRobinson.com and
Wine-Searcher (both blocked by Cloudflare), so Decanter replaced them.

## Wine sites
| Site | First screen | What it does better than our v3 |
|---|---|---|
| GuildSomm | A Rioja DO map is the hero, with a "New Maps: Spain" card on top | **Our closest competitor.** The product is the hero, and it covers Rioja, our exact subject [V: screenshot] |
| Wine Scholar Guild | A vineyard photo, a serif welcome and course dates | It gives the student their exam path straight away |
| Wine Folly | A photo of their book, plus stars and a review count | Proof as a number |
| Vinous | A magazine grid and a search bar | Search as the way in |
| Decanter | A masthead and a large "find a wine" search | One clear entry action |

None of the five looks premium. Our type and restraint already beat all of them. Only GuildSomm shows a real map.

## Premium style sites
| Site | First screen | What it does better |
|---|---|---|
| Felt | A serif headline on the left and an illustrated map on the right, with live UI cards on it | **The best match to us.** It shows the product, as a map, doing something |
| Brilliant | A serif headline and a live mini-lesson card | It shows the learning, not only the claim |
| Duna | A painted landscape bleeding into white, and one CTA | The image is the atmosphere. Nothing is boxed |
| Mapbox | A product screenshot with a logo strip under it | Proof above the fold |
| Stripe Press | One confident object, with motion | Browsing the thing is the page |

## What the best ones share that v3 lacked
1. The product is visible in the first screen.
2. The hero image bleeds to the edge, never sitting boxed under a rule.
3. Proof as numbers.
4. One primary action, with the second as a link.
5. A "live" signal, such as cards on the map or motion.

## What only we have (keep)
- Real satellite imagery with official DO boundaries, coloured by climate. GuildSomm's maps are flat illustrations.
- Every fact links to its official source. Nobody in wine shows this.
- Free, with no account, while GuildSomm and Wine Scholar Guild are paid memberships [A: their pricing not checked today].
- Editorial typography on a par with Felt and Brilliant.

## Applied in v4
- The map is the hero (Felt and GuildSomm), with a fact card carrying its source and a study card (Brilliant).
- Numbers sit under the CTA: 106 places and 10 study regions [V: the live app's Spain panel].
- "Browse the guides" is now a text link.
- "Free, no account" sits under the button.
- The sources strip keeps the logos (owner 11:47Z).

## Not applied yet, for later
- Search in the hero (Decanter). The bar already has search, so it's not needed now.
- Gentle motion: a slow pan of the hero map. That's for the build, not the mock.
- An exam-path line ("Covers Spain for WSET Level 3 and Spanish Wine Scholar") needs checking against the course outlines first [A].
