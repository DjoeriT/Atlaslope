## Title
KEEP (template) "Why Montecucco Sangiovese tastes the way it does": the standard guide title; the body makes no taste claim.

## Lede
FIX p2 "provenienti da vigneti aventi ... Sangiovese: minimo 90%": "a red wine that must be at least 90% Sangiovese" -> the 90% is a vineyard rule, and "rosso" is not on p1-p2. Now: comes from vineyards of at least 90% Sangiovese in seven communes of Grosseto province (p2 art. 3).
CUT p2 "fino a un massimo del 10%, le uve a bacca rossa": "The grape is the whole point" is editorial, and "anything else" widens the rule (only red grapes are allowed). The rules beat keeps the detail, so the lede no longer repeats it.

## place: "Seven communes in Grosseto"

KEEP p2 "comprende i terreni vocati alla qualità ed idonei alla coltura della vite"

## rules: "Sangiovese, with a little room"
KEEP p2 "Sangiovese: minimo 90% ... fino a un massimo del 10%" (title)
FIX p2 "le uve a bacca rossa": "a red wine only" -> "made from red grapes only", since "rosso" is not on p1-p2.
FIX p2 "provenienti da vigneti aventi, nell'ambito aziendale, ... Sangiovese: minimo 90%": the minimum applies per estate, so the sentence now reads "Each estate's vineyards".
KEEP p2 "fino a un massimo del 10%, le uve a bacca rossa, provenienti da altri vitigni idonei alla coltivazione per la Regione Toscana"
