# Sentence check: mosel (source mosel-spec, Produktspezifikation g.U. „Mosel“, pages 1-12)

- KEEP title (standard guide title).
- FIX p8 "Mittel- und Untermosel ... in devonische Gesteine ... eingeschnittenen engen Kerbtal": was "winds through a narrow valley cut into slate" for the whole river; the narrow valley is only the middle and lower course, and the rock is Devonian (slate is one of three rock types).
- FIX p8 "hauptsächlich (zu 50 %) SE-S-SW exponiert": "most of its vineyards face south" dropped from the lede (it is half, and the body says it).
