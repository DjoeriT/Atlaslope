Atlaslope

The five screens the flow sketch marked new, on the real map: atlas base by default (relief, rivers, towns, serif names), satellite one tap away, 3D terrain streamed live. Boundaries are the official MAPA layer; Rioja's zones come from the official municipality lists. The quiz chain is unchanged and still lives in round 2.2, linked from these pages.

  • First run · welcomeShown once: what Atlaslope is in three lines, where to start (a study region or all of Spain), how to tilt and turn the map.
  • Home · the map with TodayAtlas base, the Today card: what is due, where to continue, what to read. Close it and you are on the map.
  • Home · nothing dueThe Today card on a day with nothing to review: five new places or just explore.
  • Layers · climate onThe map menu open (bottom right, with zoom and 3D): satellite, climate, 3D relief, boundaries.
  • Place guide · RiojaWhere it sits, why it tastes as it does, fly-there buttons, a cited fact sheet, zones, neighbours, test me.
  • Progress · your mapSpain shaded in four steps by how well you know each place, counts per study region, reset with an undo.
  • Explore · Spain, the place treeThe Spain card: ten study regions with their colours and known counts. The top of the tree.
  • Explore · a regionCastilla y León: its places with their state, continue here, the rest of Spain dimmed.
  • Explore · a placeToro selected on satellite: breadcrumb, the place card with three cited facts, read the guide or test me.

Satellite imagery and terrain need internet. Type: Newsreader and IBM Plex Sans. Map: MapLibre GL. Geography: Natural Earth. Terrain: Mapzen tiles on AWS Open Data. Imagery: Esri World Imagery, attribution on the map. The climate layer is schematic by province and says so.
