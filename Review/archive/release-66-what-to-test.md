# Release 66: what to test (a calmer Guides page, map fixes)

The Guides page is a third of its old length.
Map tours have their own band.

Hard-reload first (Cmd+Shift+R).

1. Open Guides. Under the headline a black band: "Explore with the map", 15 guides.
2. Four tours show large: Rioja, Priorat, Ribeira Sacra, Getariako Txakolina.
3. Click "Show all 15 map tours". The other 11 fold out. "Show fewer" folds them back.
4. Below, every region shows all its guides as small square photos.
5. Guides with a map tour carry a champagne "Map tour" chip on the photo.
6. Tick "Show only guides with a map tour". Only those stay.
7. One "Photos:" credit line at the end of the page.
8. Open any guide. "Show map" is now a small link at the end of the line above the title.
9. On Rioja, click "Read with the map". The photo grows, then the map appears. No flash of the page in between.
