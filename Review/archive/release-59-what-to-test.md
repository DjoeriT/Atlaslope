# Release 59: what to test (Read with the map)

Rioja and Priorat now have a "Read with the map" view.
You scroll the guide and the map flies to each place it names.

Hard-reload first (Cmd+Shift+R).

1. Open the Rioja guide. Under the lede, tap the black "Read with the map" button.
2. Scroll slowly. The map should fly to each stop, with a champagne outline and the land outside a little darker.
3. At "Cool in the west, warm in the east": climate colours, a legend, and the three zone names.
4. At the soils stop: rock colours from IGME, and "Source: IGME, MAGNA 50" in the credit line.
5. Tap a small champagne dot on the map. A black card shows one sentence from the guide and its source. Escape closes it.
6. Press Escape or "Back to Rioja". You are back on the guide page.
7. Do the same on Priorat. Check the Siurana stop and the slate hills.
8. On the home page, the third number says "2 guides to read with the map". It opens the new page with both guides.
9. On a phone: the map sits on top, the text below. Credits stay readable.

Tell us if a flight feels too fast or too slow, or a stop shows the wrong place.
