# Release 34: what to test

## General (every release)
Open https://atlaslope.com/ in Chrome or Safari on a Mac or iPad. Reload once with a hard refresh if a page looks stale. Anything odd: a screenshot plus the page address and what you expected is enough.

## This release
1. **Rioja flags.** In the atlas, open Rioja. Four champagne chips: Haro, Sierra de Cantabria, Ebro, Alfaro. Open Rioja Alta: the same four.
2. **Tap a chip.** It turns black with a champagne edge. A box shows one line, the source with its page ("Pliego · p. 20"), and "Fly to ...". Fly to lands close on the place. Tap the chip again or the cross to close.
3. **Jerez flags.** Open Jerez-Xérès-Sherry. Five chips: Jerez de la Frontera, Sanlúcar de Barrameda, El Puerto de Santa María, Guadalete, Chipiona. Sanlúcar's box names both EU single documents in full.
4. **Nowhere else.** Toro, Spain and Study show no chips. Zoomed far out, the chips go away.
