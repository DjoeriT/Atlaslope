# Release 37: what to test

## General (every release)
Open https://atlaslope.com/ in Chrome or Safari on a Mac or iPad. Reload once with a hard refresh if a page looks stale. Anything odd: a screenshot plus the page address and what you expected is enough.

## This release
1. **Nine new guides.** Open each Green Spain place: Txakoli de Bizkaia, Getaria, Álava, Cangas, Rías Baixas, Ribeira Sacra, Ribeiro, Valdeorras and Monterrei. Each page tells "Why it tastes the way it does" in four beats: the place, the weather, the ground and what the rules make. A beat the pliego says nothing about is left out.
2. **Flights.** Each beat has one or two "Fly to" pills to named places, for example the Sil canyon (Ribeira Sacra), Cambados (Rías Baixas) or Bakio (Bizkaia). Each lands close on the place, with a one-line caption.
3. **Read the label.** Each place lists only bottle words its pliego defines. The words are not repeated from Famous styles.
4. **Map flags.** Select each place in the atlas. Three or four chips appear, for example Ribeiro shows Ribadavia, Avia and Miño. Tap one to see its line, its source page and "Fly to".
5. **Photos.** Cangas, Monterrei, Ribeira Sacra, Valdeorras, Rías Baixas and Bizkaia each have one or two new photos. Álava has photos for the first time: a landscape at Okondo and a glass of Arabako Txakolina, because Commons has no Álava vineyard photo.
6. **Corrected facts.** The nine fact sheets were re-checked against the current pliegos. Study questions on Green Spain no longer offer a wrong answer that shares a style name with the right one.
7. **Names.** The community names read "Principado de Asturias" and "Castilla y León", with small "de" and "y".
