Hard-reload first (Cmd+Shift+R).

2. Open Campo de Calatrava. It has guide chapters; each chapter's source tag opens its pliego.
3. Open La Jaraba's fact sheet. Merlot and Tempranillo (Cencibel) are the main grapes.
4. Search "merlot". La Jaraba is listed with "main grape: Merlot".
5. Open Almansa, Casa del Blanco, El Vicario and Finca Élez. Each has "Why X tastes the way it does" with its parts,
   a short summary and sources.
6. Open Guijoso and Pago Florentino. Same shape.
7. Open Valdepeñas. It still has no guide chapters: it comes in the last batch.
8. In Campo de Calatrava, tap a fly-there pill. The map flies to the named place and Back returns to the chapter.
