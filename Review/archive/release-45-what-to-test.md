# Release 45: what to test (Baleares guides)

Two new guides: Binissalem and Pla i Llevant (Mallorca).

1. Open both from the Guides page. Baleares shows its Mallorca satellite tile. The count goes up by 2.
2. Binissalem reads in three beats (the EU text says nothing on soil) and shows a "Vinyet" label term. Pla i Llevant reads in four.
3. Tap each map flight. It lands on the place.
4. The fact sheets read "limy" for Pla i Llevant's soils, not "chalky", and Binissalem lists the three red grapes added in 2026
   (Giró Negre, Esperó de Gall, Fogoneu).
