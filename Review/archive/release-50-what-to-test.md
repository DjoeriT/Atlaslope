# Release 50: what to test (Meseta guides, first 12)

12 Meseta places get their Terroir guide.
The Guides page gets a Meseta row with its satellite tile.
The guide count goes from 84 to 96.

Hard-reload first (Cmd+Shift+R).

1. Open the Guides page. A Meseta row lists 12 guides, with a satellite tile of the plains from Madrid to La Mancha
   and the credit line under it.
2. Open La Mancha's page. It now has guide chapters; each chapter's source tag opens the La Mancha pliego.
3. Open Vinos de Madrid's fact sheet. Albillo Real, Tempranillo, Malvar and Garnacha Tinta are the main grapes.
4. Search "albillo". Cebreros and Vinos de Madrid are both listed with "main grape: Albillo Real".
5. Open Mondéjar, Rosalejo, Los Cerrillos, Calzadilla and Méntrida. Each has "Why X tastes the way it does" with its parts,
   a short summary and sources.
6. Open Uclés, Dominio de Valdepusa, Campo de la Guardia, Manchuela and Dehesa del Carrizal. Same shape.
7. Open Valdepeñas. It still has no guide chapters: it comes in the next batch.
8. In Campo de la Guardia or Dehesa del Carrizal, tap a fly-there pill. The map flies to the named place and Back returns to the chapter.
