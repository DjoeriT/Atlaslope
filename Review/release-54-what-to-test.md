# Release 54: what to test (Guides page)

The Guides page has a row of region links at the top.
Each region shows six guides, then a link to all of them.
Every region has a new satellite tile.

Hard-reload first (Cmd+Shift+R).

1. Open Guides. Under the heading is a row of ten region names.
   Tap Andalucía. The page jumps to the Andalucía section.
2. Each region shows at most six guides.
   Green Spain ends with "All Green Spain guides".
   Tap it. The atlas opens on the Green Spain region page.
3. Look at the photos. None are cut into squares; each keeps its own shape.
   Under each region's guides is one line of photo credits.
4. Look at each region's satellite tile. It shows that region, with its own places named.
   Rioja names Rioja Alta, Rioja Alavesa and Rioja Oriental.
   Under each tile is the line "Imagery: Esri, Maxar · Spain: PNOA © IGN".
5. On a phone, scroll the whole page. Nothing scrolls sideways.
