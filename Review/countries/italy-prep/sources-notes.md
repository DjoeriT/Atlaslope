# Italy prep: sources notes (22 Oct 2026)

## Rulebooks
- [V] MASAF page https://www.masaf.gov.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeBLOB.php/L/IT/IDPagina/4625 ("Elenchi e disciplinari vini DOP e IGP") links 7z archives in the page HTML: DOP A-D (30.01 MB), E-N (22.17 MB), O-Z (29.95 MB), IGT (18.93 MB), plus "Documenti unici riepilogativi". 411 DOP, 111 IGP listed.
- [V] Link pattern: https://www.masaf.gov.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeAttachment.php/L/IT/D/<hash>/P/BLOB%3AID%3D4625/E/7z?mode=download . The hash is per archive and only in the page HTML, so a script must scrape the page for hrefs ending in /E/7z?mode=download, then extract. No per-denomination URL. Reusable by script: yes (scrape page, download 4 archives, `bsdtar -xf`; no 7z binary needed on this Mac; one PDF per denomination named "<Name>.pdf" inside "Disciplinari DOP (A-D)/").
- [V] Downloaded to data/raw/countries/it/disc/dop_A-D.7z (31.5 MB, 22 Oct 2026). Plain User-Agent worked; no login.
- [V] Barolo: disc/barolo.pdf, 9 pages (+ blank last page in text), text extracts cleanly (Word-made, 33 KB). Latest amendment listed: DM 17.04.2015, GU 97 of 28.04.2015 (correction). File created Nov 2019. [A] Possibly older than later amendments; check Barolo's page on MASAF/EU eAmbrosia for a newer single document before use.
- [V] Barolo art. 8 (page 7) lists the MGA names by name only, no municipality in the PDF. Art. 1 says delimitations are "nell'allegato in calce"; that annex is NOT in this PDF (9 pages end at art. 10). MGA-to-comune table and polygons must come from elsewhere (Regione Piemonte, Consorzio, or the 2010 DM annex in GU 241 of 14.10.2010). Open point.
- [V] Chianti Classico: disc/chianti-classico.pdf, 21 pages, PDF24 copy of Jan 2024, text extracts cleanly (49 KB). Latest amendment: DM 22.06.2023, GU 152 of 01.07.2023. Ministry header already "sovranità alimentare".
- [V] Chianti Classico art. 6 (page 9, text l.344-362) lists the 11 UGA: Castellina, Castelnuovo Berardenga, Gaiole, Greve, Lamole, Montefioralle, Panzano, Radda, San Casciano, San Donato in Poggio, Vagliagli. Only for Gran Selezione. Delimitation is in Allegato 3 (pages 18-21, comuni/frazioni/places per UGA; page 18 Gaiole/Greve/Lamole, 19 Greve/Lamole/Panzano, 20 Greve/Panzano/Radda, 21 Vagliagli). Allegato 1 p.15, Allegato 2 p.17 [A, page numbers from form-feed split, not eyeballed].
