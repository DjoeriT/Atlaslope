# Fact sheet fixes, it-south

## taurasi
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says "Ethereal, pleasant aroma"; pliego says "odore: caratteristico, etereo, gradevole più o meno intenso", p3
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: sheet says big day-night swings ripen Aglianico slowly; pliego credits regular ripening and acidity to the high clay content, and gradual ripening to temperature, rain and wind together, p6 -> "Clay-rich soils on inland hills at 400 to 700 m ripen Aglianico evenly through summer drought and keep its acidity."

## fiano-di-avellino
- applied `famous.styles`: sheet lists Apianum as a famous style; pliego p1 lists only four wines and p3 calls Apianum a 'menzione tradizionale' for the label, p3
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: sheet links cold winters and day-night swings to 'fresh' wine; pliego p6 links the climate only to gradual ripening with sugar and acidity in balance, and p5-p6 links potassium and magnesium to aromas, structure and balance -> "Clay-rich hills with volcanic ash and a climate close to ideal for gradual ripening; potassium and magnesium give intense aromas and balance."

## greco-di-tufo

- applied `famous.styles`: sheet lists Vigna as a famous style; pliego p3 makes 'vigna' a label menzione, and p1 lists only four tipologie

## aglianico-del-vulture

## castel-del-monte-bombino-nero
- applied `famous.styles`: sheet says only vinified and kept separately; pliego also requires the vineyard on the regional list ("figuri nell'apposito elenco regionale"), p3
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: sheet says cold winters; pliego gives January mean about 7 °C with lows often below 0 °C (frost) and calls June-August "periodo arido", p5 -> "Hard limestone Murge plateau with thin, stony soils, dry summers and frosty winters, planted at least 90% to Bombino Nero."

## castel-del-monte-rosso-riserva
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: sheet says a dry sub-Mediterranean climate; pliego p5 says sub-mediterraneo and subumido, arid only June to August -> "Stony limestone plateau soils, a sub-Mediterranean climate with arid summers and Nero di Troia (at least 65%), aged at least two years, one in wood."

## primitivo-di-manduria-dolce-naturale
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: sheet says climate and thin well-drained coastal soils ripen Primitivo, partly dried, into a sweet red; pliego p4-5 makes no climate- or soil-to-ripening link and p3 makes drying optional ('possono') -> "Primitivo, which may be dried on the vine or on racks, makes a sweet red on flat land between the Ionian coast and the Salento."
