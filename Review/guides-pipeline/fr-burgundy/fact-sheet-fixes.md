# Fact sheet fixes, fr-burgundy

## chablis
- applied `altitude.text`: Sheet says the slope drops 120 to 130 m and the top is under 320 m. Pliego p6 says «Le dénivelé du front de côte atteint 120 mètres à 130 mètres» and «ne dépasse pas 320 mètres».
- applied `soil.text`: Sheet says the scree warms quickly. Pliego p6 says «se réchauffant bien» (warms well).
- applied `climate.text`: Sheet says a serious risk. Pliego p7 says «un assez fort risque de gelées» (fairly serious).
- NOT applied (unsure) `famous.styles`: Sheet says it ages 8 to 10 years. Pliego p9 says «atteignant leur plénitude lorsqu'ils sont conservés de 8 ans à 10 ans» (at its best when kept that long). -> [{"name": "Premier Cru", "text": "From the best-exposed slopes, in 40 climats; best after 8 to 10 years."}]

## bourgogne
- applied `climate.text`: Sheet says 'cold winters' and 'the south sees warmer summers'; pliego says 'températures hivernales relativement basses' and southern influences 'peuvent, momentanément' bring high summer temperatures and summer storms, p11-12

## cote-de-nuits-villages
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says chalky; pliego says 'généralement carbonatés et peu épais' (limy), p7
- NOT applied (unsure) `climate.text`: sheet says continental and southern influences; pliego says 'influences continentales ou méridionales', p8 -> "Cool oceanic climate with continental or southern influences, about 750 mm of rain a year and a 10.5°C annual mean. The Côte is sheltered east of the Morvan."

## gevrey-chambertin
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says 'thin, well-drained, chalky soils high on the slope'; pliego p6 says 'sols maigres très calcaires, en haut de versant ... relativement profonds (0,50 mètre)' (limy, not chalky; 'bien drainants' applies to all soils)

## vougeot
- applied `soil.text`: The sheet says 'chalky' and 'The spec also calls them', but p7 says 'carbonatés' (lime-rich, not chalk) and p8 gives the gravel, clay and iron oxide as plain fact; vougeot-doc p7-p8

## vosne-romanee
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says very chalky high on the slope; pliego says maigres et très calcaires en haut de versant (very limy, not chalky), p6

## nuits-saint-georges
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: The sheet says a cool oceanic climate suits Pinot Noir. p9 links Pinot Noir to the east-facing slope and a 'mésoclimat chaud' with moderately fertile soils. p7's cool oceanic climate is regional. -> "East-facing limestone slopes on the Côte de Nuits, with well-drained, moderately fertile soils and a warm mesoclimate where Pinot Noir shows its full potential."

## aloxe-corton
- applied `climate.text`: sheet has typo 'Ccool'; pliego p6 'Le climat est océanique frais'
- applied `why.text`: sheet ties the cool climate to Pinot Noir's tannic structure; p9 links the tannic structure in reds only to 'forte teneur en argile, combinée à une relative pauvreté'

## beaune
- applied `soil.text`: Sheet says 'always chalky spreads' and 'stony'; pliego says 'toujours calcaires' (limy) and 'carbonatés et peu épais, bien drainants', with no 'stony', p7. Soil text is on p7, not p9.

## volnay
- applied `famous.styles`: Sheet lets any named plot follow the appellation and omits the lieu-dit harvest-declaration condition; pliego limits climats to the listed premier cru ones and requires the lieu-dit be on the déclaration de récolte, p12-p13

## meursault

- applied `soil.text`: sheet says the lower slopes and foothills are well drained; pliego p7 says only the foothill 'épandages argilo-graveleux bien drainés'

## puligny-montrachet

## chassagne-montrachet

## macon

## saint-veran
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says very chalky, pliego says "très calcaires" (limy), p6

- applied `climate.text`: sheet says warm air and shelter, pliego says "courants d'air doux" (mild) and "protège en partie" (partly), p6
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says with age it turns to all five notes, pliego says "fréquemment ... noisette ou d'amande grillée ... miel, de vanille ou de pierre à fusil" (often, or), p3/p7
- applied `famous.styles`: sheet text is 18 words with a comma splice and says 'to show where the wine is from'; pliego says the name "peut être suivi du nom d'une unité géographique plus petite", p11, and "quarantaine de noms de climat", p7
