# Fact sheet fixes, fr-bordeaux

## pauillac
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says wet, sandy or modern alluvial plots are excluded; p8 excludes only thick wind-blown sand, modern alluvium and poorly drained plots, and includes sandy-gravel soils

## bordeaux
- applied `tastes.text`: Sheet says 'Rosés: perfumed'; pliego gives 'plus colorés, plus riches et très parfumés' for the clairets only, p4 and p13

## bordeaux-superieur
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says 'fresh and stewed red fruit'; pliego says 'fruits rouges frais et confits' (candied), p3 and p11

## cotes-de-bordeaux
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says reds are 'mostly Merlot-led'; pliego says 'le cépage fréquemment majoritaire est le merlot' (often), p1
- applied `why.text`: sheet says plots were picked for drainage and ripening and 'give' round, fruity wines; pliego picks them for 'capacités de drainage et ... situation topographique relative', and those spots are 'propices' (favourable) to ripeness and round, fruity wines, p5

## entre-deux-mers
- applied `why.text`: sheet says fresh dry whites; pliego says "vifs à l'attaque" (lively), never fresh, p1/p5
- applied `climate.text`: sheet has the south-east winds evening out the seasons along with the tides; pliego credits only the "entre deux marées" position and calls the levelling "relatif", p4
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says valleys gravelly over sandy clay; pliego says "graveleux dans une matrice sablo-argileuse", p5

## medoc
- applied `climate.text`: sheet says little rain 'for the region'; pliego says 'pluviosité très mesurée pour un climat atlantique', p6

## haut-medoc
- applied `styles.text`: sheet says blends led by Cabernet Sauvignon; pliego says 'cabernet-sauvignon N est souvent majoritaire' (often the majority), p3/p8
- applied `climate.text`: sheet drops the hedge and the 'some years'; pliego says 'nivellement relatif' and 'certaines années', p6
- applied `famous.styles.0.text`: sheet ties the five to the 1855 classification; pliego p9 says only 'cinq « crus classés » au sein de la zone géographique' and names 1855 separately

## saint-estephe
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says clay soils are excluded; pliego excludes only clay soils waterlogged deep down («sols argileux à hydromorphie de profondeur»), plus sand-filled hollows and modern alluvium and sand, p8

## margaux
- applied `tastes.text`: The sheet says "very good ageing ability". The pliego says "une excellente aptitude à la garde" (p3).

## saint-julien
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says 'firm tannins'; pliego p3 says 'structure tannique' and 'couleur très soutenue', 'bouquet d'une grande élégance', p3

## graves
- applied `climate.text`: sheet says the Garonne tempers spring frost and heat; pliego says Garonne ventilation guards against heat and humidity, oceanic influences moderate spring frost, p8
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says 'coarser sands'; pliego says 'graviers plus ou moins grossiers, de sables mêlés à des limons et argiles' (coarseness belongs to the gravel), p7
- applied `styles.text`: sheet says 'Dry still reds and dry whites'; pliego says 'vins tranquilles rouges et blancs secs' (only whites called dry), p2

## pessac-leognan
- applied `why.text`: sheet says 'shielded by pine forest'; pliego says 'forêt des Landes girondines', no pine, p6
- applied `famous.styles.0.text`: sheet says '16 of them lie in the zone'; pliego says 'la totalité des Crus Classés de Graves … soit 16' lie in the zone, p8

## pomerol
- applied `altitude.text`: sheet says flat relief; pliego says 'relief relativement plat', p5
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: sheet says terraces over iron-rich clay giving a rich structure; pliego puts the iron oxides in the subsoil and says 'structure suffisante' and 'irremplaçable personnalité', p8 -> "Gravel and sand terraces over cool Tertiary clay, with iron oxides below and a kind climate, give slow-ripening Merlot its personality."

## sauternes
- applied `climate.text`: Sheet says the microclimate suits vines and noble rot. The pliego says only "un microclimat particulier" and gives the vine-suiting role to the environment, p3.

## barsac
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says limestone elements 'give' the mineral texture; pliego says 'La texture minérale des vins est liée aux éléments calcaire du sol' (linked to), p3
