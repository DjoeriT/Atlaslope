# Fact sheet fixes, it-north-east

## alto-adige
- applied `climate.text`: Sheet says swings average 15 °C. Pliego says 'di media arrivano fino ai 15° C' (up to 15 °C on average), with 'punte di 20° C' in Sep-Oct, p31.
- applied `soil.text`: Sheet says the south is limestone. Pliego says 'il terreno è calcareo ... dalla disgregazione della roccia dolomitica' (limy soil, not limestone), p31.

## trentino
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says all reds ruby, dry, full, slightly tannic and Moscato wines aromatic; pliego gives that red profile only for Trentino rosso (p8) and Moscato rosa is 'delicato, gradevole, speziato' and 'dolce' (p9)
- NOT applied (unsure) `styles.text`: sheet calls Vino Santo sweet; visible pages (p4, p7, p18) never describe its sweetness, p11-14 cut from pack -> "Dry reds, whites and rosé, mostly single-grape; also dried-grape Vino Santo and fortified Moscato."

## collio
- applied `famous.styles.0.text`: sheet says 6 in wood; pliego says "almeno sei mesi" (at least six), p8
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet gives these as all whites and reds; pliego gives them for "tipologia bianco" and "tipologia rosso" only, p5 and p7
- applied `altitude.text`: sheet says slopes mostly 15-30% and a flat exclusion; pliego says "mediamente" (p9) and lists exceptions to the exclusion (p3)
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says rooting depth 50 to 100 cm; pliego says "Mediamente ... 50 – 100 cm", p10

## friuli-colli-orientali
- applied `why.text`: sheet says the sea gently cools the hills; pliego says plain and sea 'contribuisce a mitigare le escursioni termiche' (softens swings), p10
- NOT applied (unsure) `altitude.text`: sheet says hills 'mostly' well below 200 m; pliego says 'altezze ben al di sotto dei 200 m' with no 'mostly', p10 -> "Vineyards lie between 100 and 400 m, mostly on terraced hills; the soft marl hills are well below 200 m."
- applied `famous.cite`: sheet cites lack p9; Pignolo's sale no earlier than November of the third year and the Riserva rule are Art. 7 on p9

## friuli-grave
- applied `why.text`: sheet says cooled by the Adriatic, pliego says 'effetto benefico del mare Adriatico' (no cooling), p11
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says whites pale straw, pliego says 'giallo paglierino più o meno intenso', p7
- applied `famous.styles.2.text`: sheet shows the scale by its ends (extra brut to demi-sec), pliego lists all five types, p6

## colli-orientali-del-friuli-picolit
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says 'with a slight hint of wood'; pliego says 'con eventuale lieve sentore di legno' (possible), p4
- NOT applied (unsure) `grapes.others`: sheet lists no others; pliego allows up to 15% white grapes suitable in the region, excluding Traminer aromatico, p1 -> ["other white grapes suitable in Friuli-Venezia Giulia, up to 15% (not Traminer aromatico)"]

## ramandolo
- applied `climate.text`: Sheet says winters often fall below zero; pliego p5 says "d'inverno non è raro scendere sotto lo zero termico" (not rare)

## rosazzo
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says loam-clay and mildly alkaline; pliego says 'franco-limoso-argilloso a reazione mediamente alcalina' (silty clay loam, moderately alkaline), p3
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says 'a delicate character'; pliego gives delicate for the scent only ('odore: caratteristico, delicato' p3; 'il profumo è caratteristico e delicato' p5)
