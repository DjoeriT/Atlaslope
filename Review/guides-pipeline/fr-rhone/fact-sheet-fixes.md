# Fact sheet fixes, fr-rhone

## cotes-du-rhone
- applied `famous.styles`: Sheet calls Villages a "Higher tier". p12 only says "Au sein de cette famille, ont pu être distinguées" (set apart within the family) and gives no ranking.

## cotes-du-rhone-villages
- applied `famous.styles`: The sheet says the label may add 'one of 17 named villages'. Pliego p12 lists 17 'dénominations géographiques complémentaires', and some of them are not villages (Plan de Dieu, Massif d'Uchaux, Signargues).

## cote-rotie
- applied `climate.text`: sheet says the Mediterranean influences soften the climate and the north wind is cold; p6 says only "bénéficie des dernières influences méditerranéennes", "Le vent du nord ... est fréquent", "relativement abritée"
- applied `famous.styles`: sheet says Côte Blonde soils are "often recalcified"; p8 says "assez fréquemment recalcifiés" (fairly often)

## condrieu
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says 'Deep gold' and omits balance; pliego says 'robe or aux reflets dorés intenses' and 'à l'équilibre remarquable', p1/p6
- applied `climate.text`: sheet says 'sheltered'; pliego says 'situations relativement abritées et ensoleillées', p4
- applied `soil.text`: sheet says 'stone walls'; pliego says 'murets ou «cheys»', stone not stated, p4
- NOT applied (unsure) `styles.text`: sheet states dry/off-dry as present fact; pliego dates it 'En 2010', p1/p6 -> "Still white wine only. In 2010 mostly dry, sometimes off-dry, with a tiny output of sweet wine from overripe grapes."

## saint-joseph
- applied `why.text`: sheet says south-facing; pliego p5/p7 says 'exposés sud/sud-est'
- applied `climate.text`: sheet says 'late Mediterranean influence' and 'hinders ripening'; pliego p6 says 'dernières influences méditerranéennes' (the last) and 'théoriquement peu propices à la maturité' (hedged)
- NOT applied (unsure) `tastes.text`: sheet states velvety tannins as fact; pliego p3/p9 says 'le velouté des tanins est recherché' (sought) -> "Reds: elegant and fine, with fruit, spice, liquorice or undergrowth notes; velvety tannins are sought. Whites: dry, very aromatic, with white honeyed flowers and a rich, fat texture."

## crozes-hermitage
- applied `tastes.text`: Sheet gives 'aromatic, well structured but not excessive, medium keeping' for all reds and calls the whites 'pale green'. p8 says this only of the reds 'produits au sud' and describes the whites as 'robe claire à reflets verts'.

## chateauneuf-du-pape
- applied `why.text`: Sheet says the Mistral cools; pliego p7 says it is a cold, dry wind that favours sun and cuts rain, and p9 says it reinforces the dry climate. It never says the Mistral cools the vineyard.
- NOT applied (unsure) `altitude.text`: Sheet says limestone plateau; pliego p7 says Miocene marine molasse resting on Urgonian limestone to the west, 'formant un plateau aux environs de cent mètres' -> "A plateau at around 100 metres, of marine molasse resting on Urgonian limestone."

## gigondas
- applied `tastes.text`: sheet says rosé is 'rich yet balanced'; pliego says 'capiteuse et équilibrée' (heady and balanced), p3/p8
- NOT applied (unsure) `climate.text`: sheet says the Dentelles shelter vines from the worst of the Mistral; pliego p9 says they spare the vines the drawbacks of the Provençal climate (ensoleillement et Mistral) -> "A Provençal climate of contrasts: hot Mediterranean summers and violent Mistral winds, with torrential rain possible at the equinoxes. The Dentelles spare the vines the drawbacks of sun and Mistral."
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: sheet says 'hot, dry Provençal climate cooled by the Mistral'; pliego p7/p9 gives hot summers and a cold, dry Mistral, not a dry climate cooled by it -> "Provençal climate of hot summers and a cold, dry Mistral, on stony clay and limestone molasse slopes at 160 to 400 m below the Dentelles de Montmirail."

## lirac
- NOT applied (unsure) `tastes.text`: sheet says ripe red and black fruit, pliego says "fruits rouges ou noirs, mûrs", p3 -> "Reds: dark, complex ripe red or black fruit, spice and leather, round tannins, built to age. Rosés: fruity, fresh, round. Whites: floral, garrigue and white fruit, long finish."

## tavel
- NOT applied (unsure) `why.text`: Sheet says water and heat stress 'favour structured rosé'. Page 9 says the stress can shrivel berries before phenolic ripeness for reds, 'ce qui impose une vinification en vin rosé'. Pages 3 and 8 tie the structured style to the 'plus anciennes formations naturelles'. -> "Mistral-swept, sunny, dry vineyards on free-draining soils, where water and heat stress can shrivel berries before reds ripen, forcing rosé."
- NOT applied (unsure) `tastes.text`: Sheet leaves out 'souvent dominés par des notes amyliques' (pages 3 and 8). -> "Structured, deep-coloured rosé. Young wines show very frank amylic or red-fruit notes and sometimes minerality. With age come liquorice, herbs, flint and spice. Round, very long finish."

## muscat-de-beaumes-de-venise
- applied `famous.styles[1].text`: sheet says the must is separated from the pulp before fortifying; pliego says "avant tout début de fermentation, séparé de la pulpe", p5
- NOT applied (unsure) `climate.text`: sheet says two dry seasons; pliego says "deux saisons sèches, courtes et brèves dont celle, estivale, très marquée", p6 -> "Intermediate climate with a Mediterranean tendency; two short dry seasons, a very marked dry summer, and rainy spring and autumn. The Dentelles de Montmirail shelter it from the Mistral."
