The quiz screens the plan asked to mock before building: the two doors, focus, practise any mix, the new-place intro, the reveal, Keep going, and the progress file. Same look and map as round 3. Click through from the first page.

  • Quiz · Study or reviewThe two doors on one engine. Study: what is due plus five new, with your focus. Review: practise any mix.
  • Quiz · nothing dueStudy offers five new places; Review stays one tap away.
  • FocusWhole regions or single places; new places come from here first. Clear has an undo.
  • Practise any mixReplaces the old setup screen: regions open to their places, quick picks, 10 or 20. The map follows your picks.
  • New place · the introShow, then ask: the place, its why line and one fact, then “Got it, ask me”.
  • Find it · the revealRight flows on after a second (with “Stay here”); a miss waits with the why line, “That was a slip” (undoable) and “Open Toro”.
  • The fact questionAsked once you know where a place is: grapes, styles, level or climate.
  • Round done · Keep goingAfter 20, more is due: Keep going or stop.
  • Round done · all done todayMisses link to the place; Learn 5 more, Practise any mix, or done.
  • Save or load progressFrom the Atlaslope menu: a file backup, the iPad Home Screen note. Load keeps a backup with an undo.
