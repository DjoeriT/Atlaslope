# Atlaslope quiz mock round (release 7), 17 Oct 2026

Open `index.html` in Chrome and click through from "Quiz · Study or review". Same look, runtime and map as round 3 (`../atlas-v3/`); the Explore and Progress links go there.

## What's new
- `20-quiz.html`, `20b-quiz-nothing-due.html`: the Quiz screen with the two doors. Study: what's due plus five new, with your focus. Review: practise any mix.
- `21-focus.html`: focus on whole regions or single places; new places come from there first. Clear has an undo.
- `22-practise.html`: practise any mix, replacing the old setup screen. Regions open to their places; the map follows your picks.
- `23-intro.html`: a new place is shown before it is asked (show, then ask).
- `24-find.html`: find it, then the reveal. Right moves on after a second ("Stay here" stops it); a miss zooms to the answer and your pick, gives the why line, "That was a slip" (with undo) and the place's card.
- `25-fact.html`: the fact question, asked once you know where a place is.
- `26b-round-keep-going.html`, `26-round-end.html`: end of a round of 20 with more due (Keep going), and the end of the day.
- `27-save-progress.html`: save or load your progress file, from the Atlaslope menu.

## Fixed after the fresh-eyes check (before you saw it)
The miss now zooms to the answer and names both places; the intro no longer uses up a question number; the practise list ticks whole regions properly and counts real places; "That was a slip" only after a wrong tap, not after "I don't know"; one wording for stopping ("Done for today"); the Review door explained in plain words; no duplicate Rioja in focus; the undo stays in view at large text; the next-due date is readable on the nothing-due screen.

## Still a sample
Counts, dates and scores are made up. Facts are the live fact sheets' own text. Satellite and 3D terrain need internet.
