# Copy review, PR #117: binissalem and pla-i-llevant guides (read only, facts not judged)
Verdict: fix first. 1 must-fix, 12 should-fix, 5 taste. Read both files and the guides.md rules; positioning is right (no exam framing, "why it tastes" present).
## pla-i-llevant
1. MUST-FIX, lede repeats body. Lede: "High sunshine brings concentrated aromas and high alcohol, and an integrated acidity gives the wines their structure and vigour." The embat chapter says the same ("great concentration of aromas and a high alcohol level"), and "practically at sea level" and the Embat wind appear 4 times (lede, island para, title, caption). Fix: lede "Sun and a salty wind shape these island wines: concentrated aromas, high alcohol and an integrated acidity that gives structure and vigour." Body para 2 of "embat" then reads "The high sunshine favours phenolic compounds. Reds have medium-high colour. Whites stay light in tone."
2. SHOULD-FIX, lede run-on, two ideas in one sentence plus a third. Same fix as 1 (three short claims, one sentence).
3. SHOULD-FIX, "Only land that the Consejo Regulador, the body that runs the DO, has rated fit for the listed grape varieties counts." Jargon "DO" unexplained, aside breaks the sentence. Fix: "Only land the Consejo Regulador has rated fit for the listed grape varieties counts. That body runs the DO." And drop "The vines sit practically at sea level." (repeat).
4. SHOULD-FIX, "It shapes how the vines are grown. Growers use green pruning to leave the fruit exposed to the air, and the wind adds a significant amount of salts." First sentence is empty, second holds two ideas. Fix: "The wind adds a significant amount of salts. Growers use green pruning to leave the fruit exposed to the air."
5. SHOULD-FIX, title "From fresh whites to sparkling" (styles). The body has no fresh whites; it covers grapes, yields, liqueur, sparkling, vino de aguja. Fix: "Grapes, yields and sparkling styles" or "Liqueur, sparkling and vino de aguja".
6. SHOULD-FIX, "Sparkling wines have tiny bubbles ..., and aromas of ageing on the lees. ... Vino de aguja is the lightly sparkling style, fresh and fruity with aromas from the second fermentation, and needs at least 10% alcohol. Bottling must happen inside the production zone." Five ideas in one paragraph. Fix: split to "Sparkling wines have tiny bubbles that form long chains. They have aromas of ageing on the lees. Their alcohol is at least 11%." / "Vino de aguja is the lightly sparkling style. It is fresh and fruity, with aromas from the second fermentation. It needs at least 10% alcohol." and move "Bottling must happen inside the production zone." to the island chapter.
7. TASTE, "Nineteen grape varieties are allowed. The list runs from Callet and Manto Negro to Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay. Yields are capped at ..." grapes and yields in one para; give yields their own line. "The whites smell of flowers and fruit in syrup. The reds smell of jams, raisins and nuts." fine, but "liqueur wines" is never explained for a lay reader (no fact in the guide to use; owner call).
8. TASTE, "phenolic compounds" and "calizos" unexplained; the chalk chapter is one sentence, thin but acceptable.
## binissalem
9. SHOULD-FIX, lede repeats weather chapter. Lede "climate and ground, with the local grapes, favour full ripening" and weather para 1 "Climate and ground, along with the local grapes, ... They favour full ripening". Lede's second sentence ("makes still and sparkling wine from that fruit") says little. Fix: weather para 1 starts "Full ripening brings more sugar." and drops the repeated subject; lede stays.
10. SHOULD-FIX, "Wine moved in bulk can lose quality, and here the bottling is watched by people who know this wine." Vague, reads as hype, "people who know this wine" is not a fact in the guide. Fix: "Every bottle must be filled inside this zone, in cellars on the bottlers' register. Wine moved in bulk can lose quality."
11. SHOULD-FIX, "Binissalem, one of them, shares its name with the wine. The name of each municipality can appear on a label as a smaller geographic unit, if certain requirements are met." Clumsy; "certain requirements" says nothing. Fix: "Binissalem is one of them and gives the wine its name. Each municipality's name can appear on a label as a smaller geographic unit, if certain requirements are met."
12. SHOULD-FIX, "compounds that set quality, from acids to aroma compounds and phenolics" then "fruity, structured and lasting reds." Jargon plus lists. Fix: "They also help the grapes build the acids, aromas and phenolics that set quality." and "This allows reds that are fruity, structured and lasting." (taste: keep one list, not two).
13. SHOULD-FIX, "rosados" (weather) vs "rosé" (rules, same guide). Pick one: "Rosés carry aroma and persistence." Keep "rosado" only where it is the label word.
14. SHOULD-FIX, "Moscatel de Alejandría and Moscatel de Grano Menudo, Callet, Parellada and Macabeo are on the list too, along with Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Syrah." Three stacked lists. Fix: "Manto Negro and Moll, also called Prensal, lead the list. Callet, Parellada and Macabeo are on it, with both Moscatels. Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Syrah are allowed too."
15. SHOULD-FIX, "no more than 30% Gorgollassa" appears before the reader meets the grape (not in the list). Fix: "no more than 30% of the variety Gorgollassa".
16. TASTE, "Sparkling whites are fresh, fine and lively, with intensity. Sparkling rosés are aromatic, fine and persistent." "with intensity" is empty; fix: "Sparkling whites are fresh, fine and lively." Also "The zone makes red, rosado and white wine, and sparkling white and rosé." Fix: "The zone makes still red, rosé and white wine. It also makes sparkling white and rosé."
17. TASTE, Vinyet text: one long sentence with four numbers. Fix: "A label mention for wines with lower sulphur limits. They come from lower yields: 6,750 kg per hectare instead of 9,000 kg for most varieties, and 7,500 kg instead of 10,000 kg for Parellada."
18. TASTE, "5 municipalities" appears in title, para and caption; keep two.
