The map tour inside the Rioja guide

Mock round 1, 21 Oct 2026. The guide text, charts, fact panels and photos are the real Rioja guide page from the built app. The map, the buttons and the Guides card line are design proposals. Every frame is live: tap the Map tour band in any frame of section 1, scroll in the frames of section 2, tap the filter link in section 4.

Recommended: B3. A black band under the lede with a live satellite still of the first stop that drifts slowly. It moves, so it reads as a doorway, but it keeps the lede and the first section in view. B4 pushes the guide down by a hero; B2 is the same band without the motion; B1 is what exists now and is easy to miss.

1 The "Map tour" button on the guide page

Each frame is the top of the real guide page, 1400 px wide, before tapping. Tapping any of them opens layout A (section 2). The camera drift in B3 and B4 and the pulse on the B4 button stop when the reader's system asks for reduced motion.

2 Layout A on desktop, after tapping (1400 px)

The guide column keeps every section, chart and panel. The map is sticky on the right and flies as the reader reaches each section. The main top bar with search stays on top and has no "Map tours" link. The map caption names the stop (Stop n of 9) and has a "Hide map" link.

3 Layout A on a phone (390 px)

A map strip of 240 px sits under the top bar and does not scroll; the guide scrolls below it. The strip is 240 of 844 px, about 28% of the screen.

4 The Guides page: "Map tour" line and filter link

Cards of guides with a tour (Rioja, Priorat) get a plain "Map tour" line under the reading time. A link above the region list shows only those. The tour guides are two today [V: content/stories has rioja and priorat].
