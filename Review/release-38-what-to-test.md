# Release 38: what to test

## General (every release)
Open https://atlaslope.com/ in Chrome or Safari on a Mac or iPad. Reload once with a hard refresh if a page looks stale. Anything odd: a screenshot plus the page address and what you expected is enough.

## This release
1. **Sixteen new guides.** Open each Castilla y León place, for example Ribera del Duero, Rueda, Toro, Bierzo, Cigales and Urueña. Each page tells "Why it tastes the way it does" in four beats: the place, the weather, the ground and what the rules make. A beat the pliego says nothing about is left out.
2. **Flights.** Most beats have "Fly to" pills to named places, for example the Arribes canyon, or Pintia (Ribera del Duero), where wine was drunk in the 4th century BC. Each lands close on the place, with a one-line caption. Bierzo flies only to itself: its pliego names no sight.
3. **The three estates.** Abadía Retuerta, Dehesa Peñalba and Urueña are single estates with short pliegos, so their guides are short. Their flights go to a point by the estate.
4. **Read the label.** Each place lists only bottle words its pliego defines, not repeated from Famous styles.
5. **Corrected facts.** The Castilla y León fact sheets were re-checked against the current pliegos (grapes, wine types, soils, tastes). Searching "albillo" now also finds Ribera del Duero.
