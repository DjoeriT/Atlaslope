Atlas first card: round 3, option E

Same card as E in round 2, with plainer claims and two ways to make it livelier. Titles are literal: Follow a map tour, See where it grows, See its climate, Tilt to see the slopes, Read why it tastes that way.

Headlines: 1. "See where your wine comes from" 2. "Wine places on the real map" 3. "Find the place behind the glass"

Everything on the map is the real app: the clips are screen recordings of it, trimmed to 6 seconds and cropped to the map, no interface. Numbers sit in one quiet line (106 places, 10 regions, 15 map tours, read from the tree).

E1. Motion Recommended

  • The map tour and the slopes panels are short muted loops of the real app: a Priorat tour moving between stops, and a slow turn over the Ribeira Sacra slopes (autoplay, muted, loop, playsinline, with a still poster).
  • With reduced motion set, the clips stay as their still poster and do not play. The three other panels are tight stills, with titles under them.
  • Clips: tour.webm 0.49 MB, tilt.webm 0.66 MB (VP8 webm, 800 x 500). No ffmpeg on this Mac, so I used the encoder that ships with Playwright. For production an mp4 (H.264) copy would be added for older Safari.

E2. Stills, done better

  • Every panel is a full-bleed tight crop of the real map, no interface, with its title on a dark gradient along the bottom edge. Only satellite and map images carry text.
  • The guide panel is a real vineyard photo, with its credit line under it. The Map tour button sits in the lead panel.
  • Honest limit: the imagery is a satellite mosaic, so there is no golden light to find. The crops are chosen for relief and colour instead (the red slope shading in the tour, the steep valley in the 3D crop).
