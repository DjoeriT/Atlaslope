1. First-principles review of today's card

  • The visitor's job here: start today's learning in one tap. A wine lover with no course first; a student on a course second. [A]
  • What it says: "Study or review", then "Study" again as a label. That names two buttons, not a reason to begin. [V shot 1]
  • What it should say: what you will learn, for which course, and today's first step.
  • The spine is missing: "why it tastes the way it does" appears nowhere on the card. [V]
  • Hierarchy: four similar-weight lines (start, course, focus, review). Only the black button stands out.
  • The default is "No course", and it reads as a lack: "My course: No course." Wine lovers come first (owner, 21 Oct 2026), so the default path needs the most inviting line, not a shrug. [O]
  • Course control: browser-blue radios, "Change" set as bold text, not a button. [V shot 2]
  • "Wine Scholar: every place except the three Rioja zones" explains an exclusion, not a promise. The course is also called "Spanish Wine Scholar".
  • Review: a 3-line paragraph about schedules. Its job is simple: practise any mix, schedule untouched. It can be a quiet second block.
  • Phone first: at 390 px only the heading, one line and the button fit above the fold. [V shot 1] Every option puts the button within the first screen of the sheet.
  • Desktop: the 420 px card hides nothing of Spain, but the open chooser pushes Review below the fold. [V shot 2]
