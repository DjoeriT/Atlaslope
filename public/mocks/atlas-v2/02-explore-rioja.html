Rioja Alta

Rioja Alavesa
North bank, Basque Country, under the Sierra de Cantabria. Clay-calcareous terraces, small plots.
Rioja Oriental
East, lowest, warmest and driest. Mediterranean influence, alluvial soils, more Garnacha.
